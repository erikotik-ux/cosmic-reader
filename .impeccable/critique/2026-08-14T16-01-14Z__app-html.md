## What actually moved, and why

**Error Recovery 1 -> 3 (+2)** is the largest single gain and directly attributable to the feed-failure work: the fabricated demo articles are gone, replaced by a designed error state with a working Retry.

**User Control 3 -> 4**, **Error Prevention 2 -> 3**, **Aesthetic 2 -> 3** each moved one step, tracking the contrast re-floor and the keyboard/ARIA work.

**The P0 is gone.** Run 1's blocking issue was that hero and medium cards had zero keyboard path. Measured now: **6/6 feed cards and 7/7 saved cards carry `tabindex="0"` + `role="button"`** with the headline as `aria-label` and a delegated Enter/Space handler. The Saved view no longer exposes only `Remove`.

**Five heuristics did not move at all** (1, 2, 4, 7, 10). Nothing shipped addressed them, and the score reflects that honestly.

## Comparability caveat — read before trusting the delta

Run 1 could not load the dashboard at all; it was auth-gated, so every finding was source-inferred. Run 2 measured the live DOM. The two runs are therefore **not a like-for-like comparison**:

- The fixed items are genuinely verified (keyboard 0 -> 6/6, feed failure, contrast, aria-live, aria-current).
- But run 2 also *discovered* problems run 1 physically could not see (no URL routing, Deploy Probe's silent completion, the Settings modal's missing focus trap, icon-ligature noise). Several predate this branch and would have lowered run 1's score had they been visible.

So +5 is real movement on the things that were fixed, not a clean measure of total quality change.

## Deterministic scan

19 findings in `app.html` (30 across all five surfaces), all `warning`, zero error/critical. The detector again ran **DEGRADED** — parser modules unavailable, so computed contrast, custom properties, selector matching and DOM-structure rules were never evaluated. A floor, not a ceiling.

False positives re-confirmed with code evidence: **12 of 12 `broken-image`** (5 runtime-filled `src=""`, 8 inside JS comments/regex literals), **`bounce-easing`** (`ease-in-out`, 3px travel — matched the identifier), **`marquee`** (live DOM check: `document.querySelector('.animate-marquee')` returns null; the keyframes, the class rule and the whole `updateTicker()` body are unreachable dead code).

One detector finding turned out to be **a real defect with the wrong diagnosis**: `overused-font` flagged Space Grotesk as a distinctiveness problem, but `document.fonts` reports every Space Grotesk face `unloaded` and the computed headline font is Inter. Five weights are requested from Google Fonts and **never render** — a wasted webfont request, not a taste issue.

## What's Working

1. **Undo is systemic, not decorative.** Remove-saved, hide-source and hide-topic all route through one `showToast()` with a real restore closure, including tombstone deletion from `removedKeys` so items don't resurrect from stale remote state.
2. **State-preserving in-app history.** `navigate()` pushes state, `popstate` restores with `{fromPop:true}` to avoid double-push, and the scroll container's offset survives the round trip — verified at exactly 800px.
3. **Honest failure and honest content.** The new feed error state is a real designed state, and the reading view labels itself `CURATED EXCERPT — SPACE.COM` with a View Original exit rather than impersonating the publisher.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Deploy Probe reports its result in three contradictory places, none where the user is looking
After a completed run: sidebar says `+4 detected` and `Relays 9 of 9 online` (silently down from 10 of 10), `#feed-status` still reads a timestamp 8 minutes old, the grid is unchanged, and no toast fires.
**Why it matters:** refresh is the core loop. If it cannot confirm itself, users re-click or assume the app is dead.
**Fix:** on completion update the `#feed-status` timestamp and fire the existing toast — `4 new signals — jump to top` (with the jump as the action) or `No new signals since 11:48`. Only surface a relay-count change with a reason. (The 10 -> 9 drop is technically correct: the manual refresh re-runs FEEDS only and does not re-fetch SpaceNews. It is still unexplainable to a user.)
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Material Symbols ligature text is exposed to assistive tech app-wide
129 `.material-symbols-outlined` spans, only 12 `aria-hidden` attributes in the file. Sidebar items announce as "satellite_alt My Feed", "local_fire_department Trending"; the toast live region announces "bookmark_remove Removed from saved stories Undo".
**Why it matters:** every navigation label a screen-reader user hears is prefixed with junk, and it lands inside a live region they cannot reread.
**Fix:** add `aria-hidden="true"` to every decorative icon span, or set it globally at runtime alongside the existing `enhance()` IIFE.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] No URL routing — every view is `/app.html`
`pushState(state, '')` never sets a URL.
**Why it matters:** no shareable article link, no bookmarkable Saved Stories, and a refresh mid-read returns to the Galaxy Map with the reading position lost. The clearest native-web expectation broken on this surface.
**Fix:** pass a path to the existing `pushState` call (`#feed`, `#article/<id>`) and read `location.hash` on boot. The `popstate` handler already exists.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] The Settings modal is the one unfinished dialog
Verified: opening it leaves `document.activeElement` on `<body>`, Escape does not close it (the global handler covers share/chat/detail/reading but omits settings), and the sidebar links stay tabbable behind the backdrop. The chat panel does all of this correctly.
**Fix:** add settings to the Escape handler, focus the close button on open, restore focus to the trigger on close, trap Tab — reuse the chat panel's pattern.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Filter chips have no selected state for assistive tech; source select has no label
`aria-pressed` is null on all 7 category chips (state is a cyan fill only); the 11-option source select has no `aria-label`.
**Fix:** toggle `aria-pressed` where the class is toggled, label the select, and announce the resulting count into the existing `#feed-status` live region.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P3] Primary navigation is set at 9-10px
All five sidebar nav labels are 10px uppercase; Probe Report rows are 9px. 45 visible text elements sit at <=10px.
**Fix:** 11-12px for nav labels, 10px for Probe Report values; keep the tracking, which carries the HUD feel.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

## Remaining contrast findings (measured, live)

Contrast is much improved but **not** universally passing, and one gap is a flaw in how it was previously verified:

- **Read-article dimming.** 3 combinations in `#view-feed` fail at **2.81:1** ("2m read", the `schedule` icon) and **3.93:1** (category tag), caused by a **cumulative ancestor `opacity: 0.5`** rather than the text colour itself. The earlier in-house verification composited only each text colour's own alpha over its background and did not account for ancestor opacity — so its "all nodes pass" result was measured too narrowly.
- **`.cr-map-hint` at 1.56:1** on the galaxy map. It pulses 0.4 -> 0.8 (1.56:1 -> ~9.4:1) on a 3.6s loop, and it is `aria-hidden="true"` — but `prefers-reduced-motion` pins it at `opacity: 0.4`, so **reduced-motion users are left permanently at the worst ratio**.
- `#view-saved`: **0 failing combinations.** Clean.

## Minor observations

- **`span#breadcrumb`** carries `onclick="breadcrumbBack()"` with no `tabindex` and no `role` — a navigation control still unreachable by keyboard. It was missed when the card templates were fixed. (3 of 104 onclick handlers lack both; the other two are click-outside backdrops.)
- **Trending never populated** during measurement — stuck on "Loading signals…" past 6s and 9s waits, so its cards could not be measured at all. Worth investigating as a real bug.
- **Planets have no explicit `aria-label`** — all five get `role="button" tabindex="0"` at runtime but their name depends on subtree text; the accessibility snapshot returned all five as unnamed buttons.
- **No skip link**, and focus does not move on view change: after `openArticle()`, `document.activeElement` is still `<body>` with no announcement — 9 chrome stops before content on every view.
- **The Undo toast is effectively unreachable by keyboard** — appended to end of body, focus not moved, auto-hides at 5s.
- Saved-story excerpts are 10px `line-clamp-1` and truncate mid-word.
- Article hero images are content but carry `alt=""`.
- Account menu trigger has no `aria-expanded` / `aria-haspopup`.
- **21 of 33 focusable elements are under 44x44px** at the measured desktop viewport; the card action icons (18x28, 24x24) fail on both axes. Mobile (375px) could not be measured — the panel viewport does not follow window resize.
- Dead code: `@keyframes marquee`, `.animate-marquee` and the entire `updateTicker()` body are unreachable.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the Galaxy Map is the front door, why is the feed a separate destination — and if the feed is what people actually use, why isn't it the landing view? The app currently pays for both.
2. What is Deploy Probe promising? It is the most visually dominant control in the product and its output is a 9px number in the far sidebar.
3. Why 6 articles per page when 40 are already in memory? Pagination with no network round-trip to pay for is friction without benefit.
4. The chat panel handles focus and Escape correctly; Settings does neither. Is there a shared dialog primitive, or is every overlay hand-rolled? That determines whether the fix is 10 lines or a recurring tax.
5. Is "Signal / Transmission / Relay" a brand or a tax — on the two screens where someone is trying to finish a task?
