## Design Specificity Verdict

**Genuinely authored at the surface, category-interchangeable underneath.**

Authored moments that could not be lifted into another product: the galaxy map as primary navigation (per-planet bespoke canvas rendering — the Physics node is a 250-line ring simulator with 72 seeded stars and a moon on its own orbital period); the cloaked UAP saucer wired to a real category; "End of Excerpt" as an honest statement of the publisher relationship; the Probe Report / Deploy Probe metaphor carried through to the mechanic.

Category-interchangeable: the feed itself (bento grid + chips + source select + pagination — swap palette and words and it is any SaaS dashboard), the detail view (hero image + three stat cards that exist because the layout has three slots), and the chrome. Plus fake telemetry as brand — LAT 40.7128 N / LON 74.0060 W is hardcoded New York, RELAY SECTOR-04 and Coordinate XX.XX.XX are set dressing shaped exactly like data.

The sharp version: **the map is the product's argument, and 6 of the 7 views ignore that argument.**

**Deterministic scan.** 19 findings in app.html (all `warning`); 4 home, 3 galaxy-map, 2 transmissions, 2 trending. The detector ran DEGRADED — parser modules unavailable, so computed contrast, custom properties, selector matching and DOM-structure rules were never evaluated. Findings are a floor, not a ceiling.

False positives proven with code evidence: **12 of 12 `broken-image`** (the four real `src=""` elements are runtime-filled with a three-layer fallback chain; the other eight are JS comments/regex literals, not markup); **`bounce-easing`** (the animation is `ease-in-out` on a 3px typing dot — the rule matched the identifier string); **`marquee`** (dead code, zero elements carry the class). `dark-glow` is a systemic five-hue brand token at 0.06–0.14 alpha, not slop. `overused-font` is partly misattributed — Space Grotesk is a fallback, Exo 2 is primary; Inter as body is a genuine match.

Genuinely actionable from the scan: **1** (`transition: width` on the reading-progress bar).

**Visual overlays:** not available. No user-visible overlay was produced — app.html is auth-gated and could not be rendered, so no injection was attempted against it. All app.html contrast and a11y figures below are **static/computed, not live-measured**.

## Overall Impression

The craft is real and unevenly distributed. The galaxy map, the undo system and the feed's layout integrity are the work of someone who cares. But the care stops at the mouse: the app's primary task — find and read an article — has no keyboard path from its most prominent entry point, and its most beautiful object is a dead click for the first seconds of every session.

The single biggest opportunity: **the hidden easter egg is more accessible than the primary navigation.** The UAP saucer has `role`, `tabindex`, `aria-label` and a real keydown handler. The five sector planets and every article card do not. Bringing the core paths up to the standard the joke already meets would move this from 22 to the low 30s without touching the visual design.

## What's Working

1. **The read-state and "For You" loop is real personalization, not a badge.** Consumed cards dim and check-badge via CSS class, and `openArticle()` deliberately toggles that class in place rather than re-rendering — with a comment explaining a full re-render raced `enrichOgImages()` and stranded cards on fallbacks. Matched articles carry a literal "Because you follow Physics" pill. It respects memory of what is read AND explains its own ranking, which most algorithmic feeds refuse to do.

2. **Reversibility is systematic, not ad hoc.** Every subtractive action — unsave, hide source, mute topic — routes through one reusable toast with a working Undo closure. This converts "Hide source" from a scary commitment into a cheap experiment, which is what makes users tune a feed instead of abandoning it.

3. **Layout integrity under hostile content.** `renderFeed()` branches on article count 1-6 and hand-assigns spans so every arrangement sums to rows of 12 with no orphan gaps; the hero swaps gradient treatment past 60 characters. RSS payloads are hostile, and this grid never degrades into a ragged staircase.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The highest-value cards cannot be opened without a mouse
Article cards are `<article onclick>` with no `tabindex`, `role` or key handler. A keyboard shim exists but covers only `.sidebar-link` and `.planet-node`, and runs once at load so it could never reach dynamically rendered cards. `row` and `small` cards work by accident (they contain a button whose clicks bubble); **hero and medium cards contain no button at all** and are completely unreachable. Trending is keyboard-dead. In Saved Stories the only focusable child is **Remove** — a keyboard user can delete every story and open none.
Detector agreement: Assessment B independently counted **21 non-interactive elements carrying `onclick`, 19 with no `tabindex` and no `role`**.
**Why it matters:** the core task has no keyboard path from its most prominent entry point, and the Saved view offers keyboard users nothing but the destructive action.
**Fix:** wrap card content in a real `<a href="#article/{id}" onclick="openArticle(id); return false;">`. That buys tab order, Enter, focus ring (`:focus-visible` already exists), middle-click, open-in-new-tab and copy-link-address in one edit — fixing this and heuristic #4 together.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P0] Planet clicks silently do nothing until feeds load
`openCategoryArticle()` does `if (idx >= 0) openArticle(idx)` with **no else branch**. The map is the default view, `articles` starts empty, and feeds arrive async across proxies with 12s timeouts.
**Why it matters:** the signature interaction is a no-op during the entire first-load window of every session, and permanently for any category with zero live articles (UAP will frequently have none). Users click again, and again, and conclude it is broken before they conclude it is beautiful.
**Fix:** while `articles.length === 0`, mark the map `aria-busy` and give planets the existing `.skel` pulse. Add the else branch: `showToast('No {category} signals in range yet', {actionLabel:'Deploy Probe', onAction: deployProbe})`. Toast infrastructure and the action already exist.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Total feed failure presents fabricated articles as live news
If every source fails, `renderFeed()` is never called, so `#article-grid` keeps its hardcoded demo markup — six fictional stories including a LIGO black-hole detection. The status line prints "Using cached signals (network unavailable)" **in the branch where no cache exists**, then hides after 3s. `System Status: Live Feed Active` stays lit. No retry control exists anywhere.
**Why it matters:** this is not a degraded experience, it is a wrong one. A news product showing invented headlines under a green "live" indicator has failed at the only thing it claims to do.
**Fix:** on zero articles and no cache, clear the grid and render an explicit failure state with a Retry button and last-successful-sync timestamp. Drive the footer status from the live count instead of hardcoding it. Delete the demo cards and ship the existing `.skel` skeleton as the pre-load state.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Low-alpha text tiers fail WCAG AA — both agents converged on this independently
Computed against the new `#000` section background: `text-[#dbfcff]/40` = **3.5:1** (card excerpts, 14px), `/30` = **2.4:1** (search placeholder, empty-state copy), `/20` = **1.6:1** (card dates, the **Remove** button label). AA requires 4.5:1 for body text.
Assessment B measured the same failure mode live on home.html: **18 failing nodes / 10 unique combinations**, worst **2.06:1**, and confirmed the cause is low alpha multipliers applied to otherwise-compliant tokens, not the token colours themselves.
Note on the recent black change: these tiers were already failing on the old grey; pure black made them **marginally worse** (about 3.70 to 3.52 for `/40`), because reducing background luminance also darkens the alpha-composited text.
**Why it matters:** the excerpt is how a reader decides whether to open a story. Making decision-relevant text the second-least legible thing on the card inverts the hierarchy the bento grid worked to build. And a destructive control (`Remove`) at 1.6:1 is below comfortable legibility for anyone.
**Fix:** re-floor the ramp — `/40` to `/60`, `/30` to `/55`, `/20` to `/45`, and give `Remove` at least `/60` plus a non-hover affordance. `/60` already reads correctly on `#000` and is already in use, so this is a token swap, not a redesign.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] A dead bookmark control sits on every small card
`bookmark_add` has `cursor-pointer` and a hover state but **no click handler** — clicking bubbles to the card and opens the article instead of saving it.
**Why it matters:** worse than a missing feature. The user aims at Save, gets navigation, and stops trusting every affordance on the card.
**Fix:** wire it (`event.stopPropagation()`, real `<button>`, `aria-pressed`, accessible name) rather than delete it — quick-save from the grid is the highest-leverage efficiency win on this surface.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user)** — Lands on the map every session and must click away to reach headlines; the landing view is hardcoded with no preference. Clicks a planet during load, gets nothing. Pays a mandatory extra click per article because `openArticle()` always routes through `#view-detail`, which restates Category/Source/Date he just read on the card. **Cannot middle-click headlines into background tabs** — no `href` anywhere, the single most expensive omission for him. Six articles per page, no infinite scroll, no "load more"; filtering force-scrolls to top and loses his place. No `/` to focus search, no j/k, no `s` to save.

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard only)** — No skip link. Sidebar items are `<a>` without `href` and no `aria-current` is ever set, so he can move between views but is **never told which is active**; `navigate()` moves no focus and announces nothing while the whole page changes. On the map, accessible names are computed from decorative images: the **Planets** node announces as "Physics planet Planets Worlds Beyond, button", and Missions announces as "Starship artist rendering..." — two of five sectors misnamed by their own images. On the feed, hero and medium cards are unreachable; the small card's only actuator is an `opacity-0` button with **no accessible name**. `#feed-status` has **no `aria-live`**, so six articles replacing the grid is silent, as is every filter change. The Priority Signal panel's buttons are in the tab order but positioned off-screen, so focus vanishes somewhere the ring cannot indicate. Settings and Share declare `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` but have **no focus trap, no initial focus, no focus restore**.

## Minor Observations

- `renderFeed()` uses `innerHTML +=` inside a `forEach` — re-parses and rebuilds the entire grid every iteration, discarding all prior `<img>` elements. Build an array and `join('')` once.
- Card `alt` duplicates the adjacent heading verbatim, so every card is announced twice. These images are decorative; `alt=""` is correct.
- The feed stat counter is selected by Tailwind utility classes — any type-scale change silently breaks it. The `data-stat` hook already exists; use it exclusively.
- "Categories: 4" is hardcoded and never updated. There are 7.
- Three parallel source-filter UIs (desktop/tablet/mobile) manually kept in sync — one control with responsive styling removes a whole class of drift bug.
- Empty state says "Click **Save Story**"; the button reads "**Save**".
- Sidebar says "Galaxy Map"/"My Feed"; mobile bottom nav says "Galaxy"/"Feed" — and mobile is the only nav 100% of phone users see.
- Planet imagery loads from third-party hosts (an AI-CDN googleusercontent URL and staticflickr) with **no `onerror` chain**, unlike article cards which have `imgChain`. When those rot, the map's most prominent nodes go blank.
- Detector's one genuine hit: `transition: width` on `.reading-progress-bar` animates a layout property.
- Assessment B measured **19 of 24 interactive elements below 44x44px** on home.html at 375px — worst offenders are 14px tall. home.html also has **zero `:focus-visible` rules**, versus 9 in app.html.

## Questions to Consider

1. **What if the galaxy map were the article, not the menu?** Each planet could carry its latest signal — headline orbiting the sphere — so the map is a readable surface rather than a beautiful turnstile in front of a dashboard.
2. **Why does `#view-detail` exist?** It costs every user a click and restates three facts already on the card. What breaks if cards route straight to `#view-reading`?
3. **The app tells the truth in the worst place and decorates in the best.** "End of Excerpt" is honest; "System Status: Live Feed Active", "LAT 40.7128 N" and "RELAY SECTOR-04" are costumes shaped like data. What if every one of those slots held something real — actual feed latency, source count, last sync?
4. **The easter egg has `role`, `tabindex`, `aria-label` and a keydown handler; the five primary sectors do not.** What would this app look like if the hero card were built with the care the joke got?
5. **Who is the second visit for?** Everything optimizes visit #1. A news reader lives or dies on visit #40 — "what is new since Tuesday" in four seconds. There is no unread count and no since-last-visit boundary.

## Live verification (signed-in dashboard, real DOM)

Run after the user reconnected the Chrome extension and signed in personally. These promote findings from source-inferred to observed, or correct them.

### Confirmed (observed, not inferred)

- **Keyboard-dead cards — worse than reported.** Of 6 rendered feed cards: **3 have zero focusable children**; the other **3 have exactly one focusable child, an `opacity:0` button whose accessible name is the raw ligature "arrow_forward"**. So 100% of feed cards are either unreachable or reachable only via an invisible, unlabeled control.
- **Saved Stories.** All **8** saved cards expose exactly one focusable control each: **Remove**. A keyboard user can delete their entire library and open nothing.
- **Contrast, measured on rendered DOM** (vs the computed estimates): `/20` bookmark icon **1.8:1** (est. 1.6), `/30` source+date meta **2.6:1** (est. 2.4), `/40` "2m read" and schedule icon **3.6:1** (est. 3.5). Estimates were accurate; failures are real.
- **`#feed-status` has no `aria-live`** — confirmed `NONE`. Feed replacement is silent to screen readers.
- **Sidebar links: no `aria-current` and no `href`** on all 5 — confirmed.
- **Sector naming bug.** The **Planets** node carries `alt="Physics planet"`; **Space Frontier** carries `alt="Starship artist rendering"`. 2 of 5 sectors are misnamed by their own decorative images.
- **Dead bookmark control.** 3 `bookmark_add` spans, each `cursor:pointer` with **no inline onclick, no onclick property, no tabindex, no role**.
- **The easter egg really did get more accessibility care than the navigation.** 7 `:focus-visible` rules exist in total: **4 belong to the UAP saucer**, 2 to comms, 1 generic. Zero target article cards.
- **Silent no-op code path.** With `articles` emptied, clicking a planet changed no view, fired no toast, and set no `aria-busy`.

### Corrected after observation

- **"Planet clicks are dead for the first seconds of every session" did NOT reproduce — downgrade P0 to P2.** `articles` is **pre-populated at boot** (40 immediately on every load tested, from the fallback set plus the sessionStorage cache), so the empty-array timing window Assessment A described was never observed. The silent no-op is real as a code path, but is currently only reachable for a category with zero articles — and all six categories are populated (Missions 10, AI&Tech 9, Planets 7, Moon 6, Physics 4, UAP 4). Fix is still worth doing as defensive handling, not as a P0.
- **The "JS source appears in the accessible name" observation was a probe artifact**, not a defect: `textContent` includes non-rendered `<script>` content, which real accessible-name computation skips.
